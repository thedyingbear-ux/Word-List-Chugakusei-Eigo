IPA Pronunciation Guide

Tap 🔈 to hear each sound
Stress Marks & Syllables
ˈ
Primary stress — main syllable
ˈstu·dənt → STU·dent
ˌ
Secondary stress — lighter stress
ˌen·dʒɪˈnɪər → en·gi·NEER
·
Syllable break — divides syllables
ˈstu·dənt = 2 syllables
ː
Long vowel — hold the sound longer
stiː·l → steel (long ee)
Vowel Sounds
ə
Schwa — short unstressed "uh"
əˈbaʊt → a·bout
ɪ
Short "i" — like "i" in "bit"
bɪt → bit
ʊ
Short "oo" — like "oo" in "foot"
fʊt → foot
æ
"a" in "cat" — flat open sound
kæt → cat
ɒ
Short "o" — like "o" in "hot"
hɒt → hot
ɔ
"aw" sound — like "or" in "more"
mɔ·r → more
ʌ
"u" in "cup" — short central vowel
kʌp → cup
ɜ
"ur" sound — like "er" in "her"
hɜ·r → her
ɑ
Long "ah" — like "a" in "father"
fɑ·ðər → father
Consonant Sounds
ð
"th" (voiced) — tongue between teeth, buzz
ðət → that
θ
"th" (unvoiced) — tongue between teeth, breath
θɪŋk → think
ʃ
"sh" sound — like "sh" in "she"
ʃiː → she
ŋ
"ng" sound — like "ng" in "sing"
sɪŋ → sing
ʒ
"zh" sound — like "s" in "usual"
ˈjuːʒu·əl → usual
ɡ
Hard "g" — always hard, never soft
ɡʊd → good

👋 Welcoming at the Door
Welcome! Please come in!
/WEL-kum! PLEEZ KUM IN!/
ようこそ！どうぞ入ってください！
We are so happy to have you!
/wee AR soh HAP-ee TOO HAV yoo/
来てくれてとても嬉しいです！
Let me take your bag.
/LET mee TAYK yor BAG/
荷物を持ちますね。
How was your flight?
/HOW wuz yor FLYT/
フライトはいかがでしたか？
You must be tired. Please relax!
/yoo MUST bee TYR-ed. PLEEZ rih-LAKS/
お疲れでしょう。どうぞくつろいでください！
Please make yourself at home.
/PLEEZ MAYK yor-SELF at HOHM/
どうぞ我が家のようにくつろいでください。
Can I get you something to drink?
/KAN I GET yoo SUM-thing TOO DRINK/

This is your room!
/THIS iz yor ROOM/
こちらがあなたのお部屋です！
